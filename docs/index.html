Before you get started.

Why does Windows say “Unknown publisher”?+

Cadence 0.5 isn’t digitally signed, so Windows can’t verify who published it. You may see “Unknown publisher” or “Windows protected your PC” when you open the installer. If you trust me and want to try it, use the file from the official GitHub release. If SmartScreen offers it, choose More info → Run anyway to continue. Some computers block unsigned apps entirely. Microsoft explains these warnings here.

Is Cadence free?+

Yes. This Windows 0.5 early-access release is free to download and use. There’s no Cadence subscription. Any AI agent or video-generation service you use separately may have its own costs.

Does it generate videos, or edit them?+

Cadence edits footage you already have. Make your clips with Midjourney, a local generator, or another tool, then bring the downloaded files into Cadence. Compare takes, save the best parts, and arrange them with your song in the timeline.

What does my AI agent do?+

Your agent helps install Cadence and bring your clips into the project. Tell it how you make videos and where the finished files are saved. It can import and sort those clips into named bins, and read your saved review choices. You choose what makes the cut.

Do I need an AI agent to use it?+

No. You can use the editor yourself. To connect an agent, it needs permission to work with files and run commands on your computer; a browser chat alone won’t do that. In Cadence, open File → Connect your agent and paste the connection instructions into your agent.

How do the automatic lyrics work?+

Add your song and use the included Whisper engine to transcribe it into editable lyrics with timings. It runs on your computer. Singing can trip it up, so review the words and adjust the timing where needed.

Why is the download 1.85 GB?+

It includes the editor, Whisper, its speech model, and the tools Cadence needs to run. You don’t have to set up Python or download a separate lyrics model. The download is compressed. Installation unpacks the files, which then use about 2.61 GB on your computer, plus space for your footage, previews, and exports.

What computer do I need?+

Cadence 0.5 is for 64-bit Windows 10 or newer. There isn’t a Mac version available yet. Setup uses Microsoft WebView2 if you already have it, or downloads it if it’s missing. The included lyrics engine runs on your CPU; how long it takes depends on your computer.

Are my song and footage uploaded anywhere?+

Cadence’s editing and bundled lyrics transcription run locally on your computer. Your separate AI agent or video-generation service may send data to its own provider, depending on how you use it.

Will uninstalling delete my work?+

Uninstalling removes Cadence but keeps your projects in a separate folder. Your original song and video files stay where you saved them. Back up your projects and original media together.
